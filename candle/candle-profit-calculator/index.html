How Candle Profit Is Calculated

This calculator separates variable costs from fixed monthly costs so you can see whether each additional candle contributes positively to the business.

Monthly profit = Revenue − Product costs − Percentage selling fees − Fixed per-sale fees − Advertising − Other monthly fixed costs
Contribution per candle = Selling price − Unit cost − Percentage fees − Fixed fee per sale

If contribution per candle is negative, selling more candles under the same assumptions increases the loss rather than fixing it.

Candle Profit per Unit vs. Monthly Profit

Profit per candle is useful for unit economics, but monthly profit also depends on sales volume and fixed expenses. A product can have a positive contribution margin and still show a monthly loss while sales are below break-even.

How Selling Fees Affect Candle Profit

Marketplace and payment fees usually scale with revenue, while listing or transaction charges may apply as a fixed amount per sale. Enter the fees that match your actual channel instead of assuming a universal rate.

How to Calculate Break-Even Candle Sales

Break-even sales estimate how many candles need to be sold before contribution covers monthly advertising and other fixed costs. If contribution per candle is zero or negative, there is no finite break-even point under the current assumptions.

Candle Profit Calculator FAQ

Is revenue the same as profit?

No. Revenue is sales before expenses. Profit is what remains after product costs, fees and other expenses included in your assumptions.

What should I include in cost per candle?

Include the costs that scale with each finished candle, such as wax, fragrance, wick, vessel, label, packaging and any per-unit labor or overhead you want reflected.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. Taxes and accounting treatment vary by location and business structure. This is a planning calculator, not tax or accounting advice.
