How to Use the Candle Batch Calculator

  1. Enter the finished fill weight for one candle.
  2. Enter the number of candles in the batch.
  3. Set the fragrance load recommended for your wax.
  4. Add an optional waste allowance for pitcher loss, spills and topping off.
  5. Choose whether fragrance load is based on wax weight or total batch weight.

Candle Batch Calculator Formula

Wax-weight basis

Base Finished Weight = Fill Weight × Candle Count
Base Wax = Base Finished Weight ÷ (1 + Fragrance Load)
Wax to Prepare = Base Wax × (1 + Waste Allowance)
Fragrance Oil = Wax to Prepare × Fragrance Load

Total-batch basis

Base Fragrance = Base Finished Weight × Fragrance Load
Base Wax = Base Finished Weight − Base Fragrance

Wax Weight vs. Total Batch Weight

Wax-weight basis means fragrance oil is a percentage of wax. An 8% load means 8 parts fragrance for every 100 parts wax.

Total-batch basis means fragrance is a percentage of the finished wax-plus-fragrance mixture. Use the convention specified by your supplier or recipe.

How Much Extra Wax Should You Prepare?

Use 0% for the theoretical batch amount. A 3–5% allowance is a practical starting point for many small batches, while larger buffers may be useful when you expect more pitcher loss, test pours or topping off.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much wax do I need for 12 candles?

Multiply the per-candle finished fill target by 12, then separate the finished batch into wax and fragrance according to your fragrance-load convention. The calculator performs that split automatically.

Can I calculate candle batches in grams and ounces?

Yes. Choose ounces or grams and keep your input values in the selected unit.

Is fragrance load always based on wax weight?

No. Wax-weight basis is common, but some recipes use total finished batch weight. This calculator supports both.
