Frequently Asked Questions

Which candle calculator should I use first?

If you only know your vessel size, start with the Candle Jar Fill Calculator. If you already know your tested fill weight, start with the Candle Wax Calculator.

How do I calculate fragrance oil for candles?

Use the Candle Fragrance Calculator with your wax weight and the fragrance-load convention recommended by your wax supplier.

How do I choose a candle wick size?

Use the Wick Size Calculator to narrow the starting range, then validate finished candles with controlled burn tests.

Can I use these tools for a candle business?

Yes. The Cost, Pricing and Profit calculators are designed to help model small candle-business unit economics and monthly performance.
